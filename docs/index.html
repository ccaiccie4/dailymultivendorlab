What is covered today

The latest workbook contains 27 source-derived lessons across Cisco, Fortinet, Palo Alto Networks, Arista, Aruba/HPE, Juniper and FRRouting, including seven newly researched lessons plus cumulative material from prior runs.

  • Cisco Q-in-VNI: controlled Layer-2 extension across EVPN/VXLAN, provider S-VLAN to L2VNI mapping, preservation of inner customer tags, and platform restrictions that can fail even when EVPN is healthy.
  • FortiOS 7.6.6 MP-BGP EVPN: Route Type 2/3 endpoint learning, ARP suppression, BUM replication, and the critical separation between EVPN control-plane state and recursive VTEP reachability.
  • PAN-OS Advanced Routing Engine: prefix/access/route-map filter semantics, implicit behavior, peer versus peer-group precedence, and how to isolate redistribution versus outbound-advertisement failures.
  • Arista EVPN Type-5 DCI: route re-origination across domains, independent RD/RT policy and D-path provenance as a redistribution-style loop-prevention mechanism.
  • Aruba/HPE VSX EVPN: independent BGP control-plane identities represented by a common logical VTEP, plus selective synchronization and fields that must remain unique per peer.
  • Juniper Type-2/Type-5 coexistence: route preference, tenant-table installation and Packet Forwarding Engine next-hop scaling consequences.
  • FRRouting EVPN: separation between BGP EVPN correctness and Linux bridge/FDB/neighbor/kernel forwarding state.
  • 50-question cumulative exam: interactive Check Answer controls, final grading and spaced repetition across OSPF, BGP, redistribution, MPLS/SR, EVPN/VXLAN, multichassis and RIB/FIB troubleshooting.
