1. Cisco — Understanding routed overlay networks

Official • IOS XE 26.x • Updated 2026-04-10
https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/td/docs/switches/lan/catalyst_standalones/Multihoming/multihoming-in-bgp-evpn-vxlan-fabric/routed-overlay-mh/understanding-routed-overlay-network.html

Lesson. Routed EVPN-VXLAN depends on a reliable IP underlay before tenant overlay state can forward. Treat hierarchical BGP, Ethernet-segment multihoming, L2 broadcast boundaries and gateway redundancy as separate control points. Redistribution-loop library and troubleshooting tree

Loop prevention: stamp provenance at the first redistribution boundary, reject it on return, limit prefix scope, and do not use route preference as a substitute for origin tracking.

  1. Confirm adjacency/transport.
  2. Confirm source route exists.
  3. Confirm export/redistribution policy permits it.
  4. Confirm destination protocol learns it.
  5. Confirm global RIB selects intended owner.
  6. Confirm recursive next hop and FIB.
  7. For VXLAN, confirm VTEP reachability, VNI binding, MTU and BUM state.
  8. For multihoming, confirm ESI/DF plus LACP/MLAG/VSX state.

SD-Access connection

Use the same state separation: LISP supplies endpoint/control-plane mapping while VXLAN carries the fabric data plane. Correct endpoint mapping does not prove underlay reachability, policy or edge forwarding.
